Our mission

A place where faith, family, and service grow together.

Masjid Ar-Rahman is rooted in the values of worship, knowledge, and community care. We are a gathering place for Muslims across generations, with a focus on helping families build strong iman, strong bonds, and meaningful connections.

We believe a masjid is more than a building. It is a space for remembrance, support, and action, where the rhythm of prayer meets the needs of the people around us.

Our aim

To create a welcoming, spiritually uplifting environment where every visitor feels seen, supported, and encouraged to grow.
